# Intentions start causal processes, not promised outcomes

Semantic actions such as cooking, cleaning, sleeping, building or providing a service never carry a precomputed final mutation. An accepted `Intention` may start a durable `ControlEpisode`, but physical, biological, neural and digital mechanisms determine each committed transition from current state and feedback. The episode may pause, fail, replan or end with partial and unintended consequences; duration is an estimate, not a completion contract. This rejects convenient action functions and delayed atomic completions in favor of canonical closed-loop causality and deterministic replay.
